1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Beta Tech Labs Company Limited (the Company) seeks to prevent money laundering, terrorist financing, and related abuse of the Rowan platform, including the wallet, peer-to-peer cash conversion, and utilities features.

2. Scope

This policy applies to directors, employees, and contractors with access to user or transaction information; to product features covering registration, identity verification, cash-out, buy, utilities, trader operations, and administration tools; and to third parties that process identity or payments on the Company’s instruction.

3. Roles and responsibilities

The board of directors approves this policy at least annually and ensures adequate resources.

The Compliance Officer is responsible for day-to-day customer due diligence standards, escalations, coordination of suspicious transaction reports, and staff training records.

Product and engineering teams implement tier limits, audit logging, and any automated screening tools approved by management.

Support and operations staff execute identity reviews, freezes, and user communication consistent with this policy.

4. Product risk summary

Conversion of digital assets to mobile money (cash-out) and the reverse (buy) present the highest residual risk and are controlled through tiered identity verification, amount and velocity limits, and trader float discipline.

Structuring of many small conversions, synthetic identity, sanctioned persons, and partner misuse are mitigated through monitoring, manual review, written partner contracts, and, over time, enhanced automated identity tools.

The Company maintains a low risk appetite for unmonitored high-volume activity, known politically exposed persons without enhanced due diligence, and activity involving jurisdictions on its restricted list when multi-country services are introduced.

5. Customer due diligence

5.1 Information collected

Depending on tier, the Company may collect and retain: full legal name; verified telephone number; date of birth where collected; nationality or residence; government identity document type and number; document images; selfie or liveness evidence when an automated provider is live; and Stellar public key or wallet identifiers.

5.2 Tiers

NONE. Limited onboarding. Access is restricted as configured in the product. Significant cash conversion should not be available once management adopts a verification-first rule.

BASIC. Government identity (or passport) and approval after review (manual or automated). Unlocks standard cash-out and buy within programmed limits.

VERIFIED. Additional information such as address or source of funds when required. Unlocks higher limits or business use as configured.

Recommended standard for pilot cash conversion features: minimum BASIC verification before cash-out or buy.

Transaction limits are enforced in application configuration and must remain aligned with this policy whenever either is changed.

5.3 Enhanced due diligence

Enhanced due diligence, lower limits, or freeze is considered for politically exposed persons and close associates; sanctions or watchlist matches; inconsistent identity evidence; unusual velocity relative to profile; and other elevated risk indicators identified by operations.

5.4 Refusal and exit

The Company may refuse onboarding, refuse transactions, reverse where lawful, or terminate accounts where information is incomplete or false; where sanctions or terrorist financing risk is present; where money laundering or fraud is suspected; or where terms of service or acceptable use are breached.

6. Ongoing monitoring

As a minimum the platform shall: block or hold activity that exceeds tier limits; flag repeated similar off-ramps in short periods; flag new accounts exhibiting maximum-tier behaviour; log administrative freezes and identity decisions with actor and timestamp; and review open flags on a defined schedule during pilot operations (for example daily).

7. Sanctions and prohibited use

Prohibited activities and sanctions practices are set out in the Acceptable Use and Sanctions Policy. The Company does not knowingly provide services where prohibited by applicable sanctions law or by its internal lists.

8. Record retention

Customer due diligence records, transaction records, and monitoring materials are retained for at least five years after the end of the business relationship, or longer if required by law, under secure access control.

9. Suspicious transaction reporting

Staff escalate potential suspicion to the Compliance Officer in writing through the designated ticket or email channel. The Compliance Officer reviews within the agreed service level. If suspicion remains, a report is filed with the Financial Intelligence Authority of Uganda (or through the channel directed by counsel). The subject of the report must not be tipped off. 10. Training

Personnel with production system access receive anti-money laundering awareness on appointment and at least annually. Completion is recorded.

11. Third parties

Payment, identity, and utility vendors operate under written contracts that address confidentiality, security, and, where appropriate, audit rights. Overall customer risk decisions on the Rowan platform remain with the Company.

12. Review

This policy is reviewed at least annually and after material product or legal change.
