This policy applies to end users of Rowan, peer-to-peer traders, and, where relevant, parties accessing Rowan systems for integration. It supplements the Terms of Service and the Trader Agreement.

1. Acceptable use

Rowan may be used only for lawful personal or business purposes consistent with the product features: digital asset wallet functions, peer-to-peer conversion with approved traders, and utilities via approved providers.

2. Prohibited activities

Without limitation, the following are prohibited:

Use of the Service for money laundering, terrorist financing, or proliferation financing.
Fraud, scams, identity theft, or synthetic identities.
Sanctions evasion or dealing with blocked parties.
Markets for illegal drugs, illegal weapons, or child sexual abuse material.
Unlicensed gambling where illegal under applicable law.
Tax evasion schemes.
Market manipulation or wash trading involving traders.
Abuse of refunds or disputes.
Attacks on infrastructure, malware, or credential stuffing.
Harvesting personal data from the platform for unauthorised purposes.

3. Sanctions screening

Beta Tech Labs may screen names, identity attributes, and related data against applicable sanctions and watchlists, whether maintained internally or by a service provider.

If screening produces a match or unresolved high risk, the Company may freeze accounts, refuse onboarding, block transactions, and report activity where the law requires. Users and traders must not obfuscate identity to evade screening.

4. Restricted locations

The Company may refuse service where the product is not offered or where law prohibits service. Geographic or other access restrictions may change without individual prior notice beyond technical or in-application restrictions.

5. Enforcement

The Company may investigate, request additional information, limit features, suspend access, or terminate relationships. Detailed reasons may be withheld where disclosure would compromise security, an investigation, or tip-off prohibitions.
