This Agreement is separate from the end-user Terms of Service. Electronic acceptance in the trader application constitutes signature. Counsel should finalise commission language, liability, and independent contractor classification before use in production.

Effective date: the date the Platform approves the Trader account on Rowan.

1. Role of the Trader

The Trader provides liquidity and local payment fulfilment for Rowan peer-to-peer buy and sell (cash-out) orders in supported corridors, initially Uganda mobile money on networks supported by the product (for example MTN and Airtel).

On a sell order, the user locks USDC in escrow and the Trader sends the quoted mobile money amount to the user’s number, then confirms only after the transfer is true. On a buy order, the user sends mobile money to the Trader as instructed and the Trader releases USDC after confirming receipt.

The Trader is an independent contractor and not an employee, partner, or general agent of the Platform. The Trader has no authority to bind the Platform except as expressly authorised in writing. The Trader operates their own mobile money float and USDC inventory at their own risk. Rowan does not prefund the Trader’s UGX wallet.

2. Eligibility

The Trader must complete Platform onboarding and any identity or business verification requested by the Platform (which may include government identity documents, mobile money ownership checks, and, where requested, evidence of prior peer-to-peer trading history); maintain accurate telephone numbers, mobile money wallets, and Stellar details; maintain sufficient float in both local currency and USDC for the ads or availability the Trader posts; and must not be restricted under applicable sanctions lists or Platform policies.

3. Operational standards

The Trader shall respond to matched orders within Platform time limits; send only the correct amounts to the correct numbers; upload or confirm proofs where the product requires; confirm receipt or release only when the underlying fact is true; use only authenticated applications and devices; and not share login credentials.

The Platform may reassign orders, apply timeouts, adjust rankings, suspend access, or terminate the Trader for late, incomplete, or dishonest fulfilment.

4. Fees and settlement

User fees and spreads charged by the Platform are distinct from trader earnings. The Trader’s earn rate is as displayed in the application or in a written schedule from the Platform. Restoration of float, release of digital assets, and clawbacks follow the Platform’s state machines and dispute outcomes. The Trader is solely responsible for tax on earnings.

5. Disputes

Disputes with users are handled under Platform dispute rules. The Trader must provide evidence promptly. The Platform may release escrow, reverse float, or freeze accounts based on the evidence. Fraudulent claims by either side may result in permanent exclusion.

6. Compliance

The Trader must not process orders for customers the Trader knows or suspects are engaged in money laundering or other illegal activity. The Trader must cooperate with lawful information requests from the Platform for anti-money laundering or fraud review. The Trader must not fabricate trades or layer activity to manipulate systems.

7. Confidentiality and personal data

User telephone numbers and order details disclosed for a job are confidential and may be used only to complete that fulfilment. The Trader must not store user numbers for marketing, spam users, share lists offline, or contact users off-platform to circumvent escrow. After the order is complete or cancelled, the Trader must not reuse the number except as needed for a bona fide dispute on that same order.

8. Prohibited conduct

The following are prohibited: collusion with users to defraud the Platform; false proofs; deliberate delay to extract better rates; solicitation of off-platform deals that circumvent escrow on Platform-originated jobs; and multi-accounting to game rankings or limits.

9. Suspension and termination

The Platform may suspend or terminate trader access for risk, compliance, or policy reasons. The Trader may resign by written notice and must complete or hand over open jobs as directed. Outstanding float and escrow will be settled under product rules.

10. Liability

The Platform is not responsible for the Trader’s network outages, mobile money downtime, or errors in numbers or amounts made by the Trader. Liability of the Trader to the Platform is not limited for fraud or wilful misconduct. The Platform’s liability to the Trader is limited as counsel-aligned with overall product terms, excluding cases of Platform fraud or wilful misconduct.

11. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Uganda. Disputes shall be resolved in the courts of [Kampala / as directed by counsel], unless the parties later agree to mediation or arbitration in a written schedule.
