1. Who we are

Beta Tech Labs Company Limited operates Rowan. Questions about personal data may be sent to the privacy contact above.

2. Personal data we collect

Identity data. Full name, telephone number, date of birth where provided, nationality, identity document type and number, document images, and selfie or liveness images when we use an automated verification provider.

Account data. User identifiers, Stellar address, preferences, and application version.

Transaction data. Quotes, amounts, rails, counterparties as relevant, transaction hashes, statuses, and dispute materials.

Communications. Support messages and technical metadata related to one-time codes.

Technical data. Internet protocol address, security logs, and approximate location if your device permissions allow.

Partner-supplied status. Results returned by identity verification or payment providers acting on our behalf.

We obtain data that you submit, data generated by your use of Rowan, and data from verification or fulfilment partners.

3. Purposes of processing

We process personal data to provide the Service under our contract with you; to meet anti-money laundering, fraud prevention, and sanctions obligations; to secure and improve the Service; to handle support and disputes; and, where required by law, to respond to authorities. Marketing, if any, is based on consent where the law requires consent.

We do not sell personal data.

4. Sharing

We share personal data only as needed with infrastructure providers (hosting, messaging); public blockchain networks (data written on-chain is public by nature of the network); identity verification providers when connected; payment and utility partners for fulfilment; professional advisers; and public authorities when legally required.

Partners are expected to protect data under contract and applicable law.

5. International transfers

Servers or partners may process data outside Uganda. Where the law requires, we implement appropriate safeguards for such transfers as advised by counsel.

6. Retention

We keep personal data for as long as your account remains active and thereafter as needed for anti-money laundering record-keeping (commonly at least five years after the end of the relationship), disputes, and legal claims. We then delete or anonymise data where feasible and lawful.

7. Security

We apply access controls, encryption in transit, secret management, and monitoring appropriate to a financial technology service. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Report suspected compromise to [SECURITY CONTACT].

8. Your rights

Subject to Uganda law, you may request access to your personal data; correction of inaccurate data; deletion where not blocked by retention duties or other law; and restriction or objection in cases recognised by law. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw consent without affecting prior lawful processing.

Contact the privacy email above. You may also lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Office of Uganda.

9. Children

Rowan is not directed at children below the age at which financial contracts may lawfully be formed. We do not knowingly onboard under-age users.

10. Local storage

Applications and websites may use local storage or similar technologies for session continuity, preferences, and security. Essential storage is required for the Service to function.
