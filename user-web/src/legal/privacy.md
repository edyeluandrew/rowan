This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share personal data when you use Rowan. Register as a data controller or processor with the Personal Data Protection Office if counsel advises.

1. Who we are

Beta Tech Labs Company Limited operates Rowan. Questions about personal data may be sent to support@rowanpay.app.

2. Personal data we collect

Identity data. Full name, telephone number, date of birth where provided, nationality, identity document type and number, document images, and selfie or liveness images when we use an automated verification provider.

Account data. User identifiers, Stellar public address, preferences, and application version. We store a hash of your telephone number for matching and security; the number itself is used to create your account, send one-time codes, and complete trades.

Transaction data. Quotes, amounts, rails, counterparties as relevant, transaction hashes, statuses, and dispute materials (including payment proofs you upload).

Communications. Support messages and technical metadata related to one-time codes.

Technical data. Internet protocol address, security logs, and approximate location if your device permissions allow.

Partner-supplied status. Results returned by identity verification or payment and utility providers acting on our behalf.

We obtain data that you submit, data generated by your use of Rowan, and data from verification or fulfilment partners.

We do not collect your recovery phrase or private keys. Those stay on your device. Biometric unlock, where offered, uses your device biometrics and is not uploaded to our servers.

3. Purposes of processing

We process personal data to provide the Service under our contract with you; to match you with traders and complete buy, sell, and utility orders; to meet anti-money laundering, fraud prevention, and sanctions obligations; to secure and improve the Service; to handle support and disputes; and, where required by law, to respond to authorities. Marketing, if any, is based on consent where the law requires consent.

We do not sell personal data.

4. Sharing

We share personal data only as needed with:

Infrastructure providers (hosting, messaging, storage).

Public blockchain networks. Amounts, addresses, memos, and transaction hashes written on Stellar are public by nature of the network.

Matched peer-to-peer traders. For an active buy or sell order we share the telephone number and order details the trader needs to send or confirm mobile money. Traders must use that information only to complete that order.

Utility and payment partners. For airtime, data, and bills we share the destination number, meter or account reference, amount, and related identifiers with the fulfilment partner (currently including MarzPay for Uganda utilities).

Identity verification providers when connected.

Professional advisers, and public authorities when legally required.

Partners are expected to protect data under contract and applicable law.

5. International transfers

Servers or partners may process data outside Uganda. Where the law requires, we implement appropriate safeguards for such transfers as advised by counsel.

6. Retention

We keep personal data for as long as your account remains active and thereafter as needed for anti-money laundering record-keeping (commonly at least five years after the end of the relationship), disputes, and legal claims. We then delete or anonymise data where feasible and lawful.

7. Security

We apply access controls, encryption in transit, secret management, and monitoring appropriate to a financial technology service. Phone numbers used for login are hashed at rest where the product stores a hash. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Report suspected compromise to support@rowanpay.app.

8. Your rights

Subject to Uganda law, you may request access to your personal data; correction of inaccurate data; deletion where not blocked by retention duties or other law; and restriction or objection in cases recognised by law. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw consent without affecting prior lawful processing.

Contact support@rowanpay.app. You may also lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Office of Uganda.

9. Children

Rowan is not directed at children below the age at which financial contracts may lawfully be formed. We do not knowingly onboard under-age users.

10. Local storage

Applications and websites may use local storage or similar technologies for session continuity, preferences, and security. Essential storage is required for the Service to function. Wallet keys and recovery phrases stored locally remain under your control.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The last updated date will change. Material changes may be notified in the application.
